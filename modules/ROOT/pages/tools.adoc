= Sending Alert Notifications to External Systems
:page-info-pills: anypoint-pricing-titanium
:description: Use MuleSoft Anypoint Monitoring to configure alert notification connectors — including ServiceNow, Slack, Salesforce, Jira, and Zendesk — and send custom operational and business metrics to Anypoint Monitoring.
:keywords: MuleSoft, Anypoint Monitoring, external notifications, ServiceNow, Slack, Salesforce Connector, Jira, Zendesk, alert connectors, Anypoint Exchange, custom metrics

Anypoint Monitoring provides links to connectors on Anypoint Exchange that you can incorporate into a Mule app to deliver alert notifications to external systems.

. In the sidebar, click *Tools*.

You can hover over a connector link to learn more before navigating to Anypoint Exchange.

== Supported Connectors

The *Tools* page links to these connectors on Anypoint Exchange:

* https://anypoint.mulesoft.com/exchange/com.mulesoft.connectors/mule-servicenow-connector/[ServiceNow]
* https://anypoint.mulesoft.com/exchange/com.mulesoft.connectors/mule-salesforce-connector/[Anypoint Connector for Salesforce (Salesforce Connector)]
* https://anypoint.mulesoft.com/exchange/com.mulesoft.connectors/mule4-jira-connector/[Jira]
* https://anypoint.mulesoft.com/exchange/com.mulesoft.connectors/mule4-zendesk-connector/[Zendesk]
* https://anypoint.mulesoft.com/exchange/com.mulesoft.connectors/mule4-slack-connector/[Slack]
* https://anypoint.mulesoft.com/exchange/com.mule.modules/mule-custom-metrics-extension/[Anypoint Monitoring Custom Metrics Connector]
+
Subscription requirements apply. For information about custom metrics, see xref:anypoint-custom-metrics-connector.adoc[].
