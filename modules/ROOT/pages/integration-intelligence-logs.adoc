= Getting Logs with Integration Intelligence
:page-info-pills: anypoint-pricing-titanium

Troubleshoot, analyze, and retain logs for your Mule apps and APIs in Integration Intelligence.

image::ii-logs.png["Integration Intelligence Logs"]

[calloutlist]
. Integration Intelligence in the Salesforce Integration app
. *Logs* in Integration Intelligence
. Log count over time
. Filters for selecting a <<lookback,time period>>, specific log levels, loggers, and Mule apps 
. Logs by timestamp, log level, app, logger, and log message

[[supported-deployment-types]]
== Supported Deployment Types

For logs, Integration Intelligence supports Mule apps and Mule APIs deployed to these hosts:

* CloudHub 2.0 
* Runtime Fabric

For dashboards in Integration Intelligence, see xref:integration-intelligence-dashboards.adoc#supported-deployment-types[Supported Deployment Types (for metrics in dashboards)].

For information about hosts, see xref:hosting-home::index.adoc#runtime-plane-hosting-options[Runtime Plane Hosting Options].

=== Navigate to Logs from the Salesforce App Launcher

Integration Intelligence resides in the Integration app. If you're connected to the Salesforce org that supports your MuleSoft telemetry data, you can navigate directly to your Integration Intelligence logs from the App Launcher:

// Meet prerequisites. 
. Meet the prerequisites in <<prereqs>>.

. From the App Launcher, find and select the *Integration* app. 
+

. From the Integration Intelligence sidebar, click *Logs*.

== Check Logs

Use *Logs* in Integration Intelligence to troubleshoot issues. 

[IMPORTANT] 
====
After initial ingestion, data can take up to 10 minutes to appear in Integration Intelligence. The first time you open *Logs*, it can take a few additional minutes for events and log entries to load.
====

. Navigate to *Logs*.
+
For guidance see <<nav-ii>>.
. Optionally, narrow the scope of logs:
