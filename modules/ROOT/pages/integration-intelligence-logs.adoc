[[nav-salesforce]]
=== Navigate to Logs from the Salesforce App Launcher

Integration Intelligence resides in the Integration app. If you're connected to the Salesforce org that supports your MuleSoft telemetry data, you can navigate directly to your Integration Intelligence logs from the App Launcher:

// Meet prerequisites. 
. Meet the prerequisites in <<prereqs>>.
. From your Salesforce environment, click image:ii-app-launcher-icon.png["",20,20] (App Launcher). 
. From the App Launcher, find and select the *Integration* app. 
+
The launcher opens to the app's *Intelligence* tab.
. From the Integration Intelligence sidebar, click *Logs*.
. <<check-logs, Check your logs>>.

[[check-logs]]
== Check Logs

Use *Logs* in Integration Intelligence to troubleshoot issues with your Mule apps. 

. Navigate to *Logs*.
+
For guidance see <<nav-ii>>.
. Optionally, narrow the scope of logs:

//From Anshuman on 10/27:
* *Log Levels* includes *WARN*, *ERROR*, or *FATAL*.
// TODO: Operation Name -> Logger (where do the names of the loggers come from?)
// See https://docs.mulesoft.com/monitoring/logs-us-eu#filters_predefined. (EU/US log search)
* *Logger*: Log4j logger class, such as `HttpListenerRegistry`
// TODO: Service Name -> Application (where do the names of the loggers come from?) or "App"?
* *Application*: Name of the Mule app that produces the logs. 

. Review the log message. 
+
For long messages, you can click the message to review the entire message.
