= Setting Up Custom Dashboards
:page-info-pills: anypoint-pricing-core
:page-aliases: create-custom-dashboard.adoc custom-dashboard-alerts.adoc

In the https://anypoint.mulesoft.com/monitoring[US] and https://eu1.anypoint.mulesoft.com/monitoring[EU] clouds, create and configure custom dashboards to monitor metrics on your Mule apps and APIs. 

[[view]]
== View a Custom Dashboard

Find and open a custom dashboard in Anypoint Monitoring.

image::dashboard-custom-home-hf.png["Custom dashboards"]

To view a custom dashboard:

. Navigate to Anypoint Monitoring:
+
Click *Monitoring* from the Anypoint Platform home page or from the *Runtimes* menu in the header.
. In the sidebar, click *Custom Dashboards* to display custom dashboards.
. Search for a custom dashboard by name or tag.
. Click a name in the list to open the custom dashboard.
+
Custom dashboards share common properties:
+
image::dashboard-custom-hf.png["Custom dashboard example"]
+
--
[calloutlist]
. Configurable name of the custom dashboard
. Configurable title of a row in custom dashboard
+
You can add multiple rows to a custom dashboard. Rows can contain multiple charts (also called panels or widgets).
. Graphs in a custom dashboard row
+
You can create graphs, singlestat charts, tables, and text panels for your custom dashboard. For more information, see <<charts_adding>>.
. Lookback window, refresh options, and other dashboard settings:

* Zoom in or out.
* Set a time or date range for the lookback period.
* Set an autorefresh period, or manually refresh at any time.
* Favorite your dashboard.
* View in full screen mode.
* Duplicate or delete your dashboard.
--

[[config_dashboard]]
== Set Up a Custom Dashboard

Create and configure a custom dashboard in Anypoint Monitoring.

image::dashboard-custom-config.png["Custom dashboard configuration"]

[calloutlist]
. Clicking image:dashboard-custom-config-icon.png["",20,20] (pencil) opens the dashboard configuration window.

. *Dashboard settings* tabs:

* *General*: For configuring a unique name and description, a time zone (local or UTC), tags, and a tooltip style for your dashboard
* *Rows*: For configuring rows for the charts in your dashboard
+
See <<rows>>. 
* *Metadata*: For viewing dashboard metadata, including creation dates, updates, and versioning
+
See <<metadata>>. 
. Charts (also called panels or widgets) you can add to your custom dashboard:
* *Graph*: Display metrics over time.
* *Singlestat*: Provide a summary statistic (a single total or number) for time-series data, such as a response time or a heap usage statistic.
* *Table*: Display time-series data in a tabular format. You can change cell coloring, date, and value formatting.
* *Text*: Add a panel with textual content in Markdown or HTML.
+
See <<charts_adding>>.

Set up a new dashboard:

. Navigate to Anypoint Monitoring:
+
Click *Monitoring* from the Anypoint Platform home page or from the *Runtimes* menu in the header.
. In the sidebar, click *Custom Dashboards* to display custom dashboards. 
. Click *New Dashboard*.
. Click image:dashboard-custom-config-icon.png["",20,20] to open the dashboard configuration window.
. Provide a name and other general dashboard settings.
+
image::dashboard-custom-config-general-hf.png["Dashboard general configurations"]
+
[%header,cols="1,4"]
|===
2+| General Dashboard Settings
| *Name* | Name for your dashboard.
| *Description* | Description of your dashboard.
| *Tags* | A short identifier that can help you locate a dashboard in the custom dashboard list.
| *Timezone* a|

Most graphs track data over time, so your time options are important. Your options are:

* *Local browser time*: (Default) Time taken from the system time of the computer that the browser is running on.
* *UTC*: Coordinated Universal Time standard, which uses a 24 hour cycle (for example, `19:20`) and corresponds to GMT.
| *Graph Tooltip* a| Options include: 

* *Default*: The crosshair cursor appears only in the chart that you hover over.
* *Shared Crosshair*: The crosshair appears on all charts.
* *Shared Tooltip*: The crosshair and the tooltip appear on all charts.
|===
. Add graphs, singlestat charts, tables, or text panels to a row in your custom dashboard.
+
For guidance, see <<charts_adding>>.
. Optionally, click *Rows* to manage the row that contains your chart.
+
You can provide a title for a row and make the title visible in the dashboard. You can also create additional rows for your charts. See <<rows>>.

[[charts_adding]]
== Add Charts to a Custom Dashboard

Charts are panels (or widgets) you can add to rows in custom dashboards to display metrics on your Mule apps or APIs.

Add a chart to your dashboard:

. From *Custom Dashboards*, open your custom dashboard.
+
For guidance, see <<view>>.
. If you need a new row for your chart, scroll down to find and click *Add Row*.
+
For row configuration options, see <<rows>>.
. To add a chart to the row, click *Add*.
+
image::dashboard-custom-widgets.png["Custom dashboard widgets"]
+
If chart icons aren't visible in your row:
+
.. Hover over the row to display its image:more-menu-vert-ellipsis.png["",15,15] (*More menu*) for the row, and click *+ Add widget*. 
+
This menu appears at the top of the row.
.. Click one of the chart types for your row.
. Hover over the chart to display its image:more-menu-vert-ellipsis.png["",15,15] (*More menu*), and click *Configure*.
. To configure the chart in a custom dashboard, see:
+
* xref:dashboard-custom-config-graph.adoc[Configuring graphs]
* xref:dashboard-custom-config-singlestat.adoc[Configuring singlestat charts]
* xref:dashboard-custom-config-table.adoc[Configuring tables]
* xref:dashboard-custom-config-text.adoc[Configuring text panels]

[[rows]]
== Manage Custom Dashboard Rows

Manage dashboard rows from the dashboard configuration window. 

. From *Custom Dashboards*, open your custom dashboard.
+
For guidance, see <<view>>.
. Click image:dashboard-custom-config-icon.png["",20,20] to open the dashboard configuration window.
. In *Dashboard Settings*, click *Rows* to configure your dashboard rows.
+
image::dashboard-custom-config-rows-hf.png["Dashboard row configuration options"]
+
[%header,cols="1,4"]
|===
2+| Row Configuration Options for Custom Dashboards
| Title | Title for your row.
| Show Title | Make the title visible to end users.
| Up Arrow icon | Move a row up in the dashboard when there is more than one row in the dashboard.
| Down Arrow icon | Move a row down when there is more than one row in the dashboard.
| Trashcan icon | Delete the row and any charts it contains from the dashboard.
|===

[[metadata]]
== Get Custom Dashboard Metadata

Get information about creation dates, updates, and the custom dashboard version. 

. From *Custom Dashboards*, open your custom dashboard.
+
For guidance, see <<view>>.
. Click image:dashboard-custom-config-icon.png["",20,20] to open the dashboard configuration window.
. In *Dashboard Settings*, click *Metadata* to review your dashboard information.
+
image::dashboard-custom-config-metadata-hf.png["Dashboard metadata"]
+
[%header,cols="1,4"]
|===
2+| Metadata Available for Custom Dashboards
| *Last Updated at* | Date and time of last update to the dashboard.
| *Last Updated by* | User who made the last update.
| *Created at* | Date and time the dashboard was created.
| *Created by* | Username of person who created the dashboard.
| *Version* | Version number of the dashboard. The version increments each time you save modifications to the dashboard.
|===

[[dup_dashboard]]
== Duplicate a Custom Dashboard

Create a copy of a dashboard. The copy requires a unique name but retains the configuration and all charts. You can modify that configuration as needed.

. From *Custom Dashboards*, open a custom dashboard.
+
For guidance, see <<view>>.
. From the dashboard's header, click image:more-menu-vert-ellipsis.png["",15,15] (*More menu*), and select *Duplicate*.
. Save a *New Name* for the dashboard.
+
Your copy of the dashboard opens.

[[delete_dashboard]]
== Delete a Custom Dashboard

Delete a custom dashboard you no longer need.

. From *Custom Dashboards*, open a custom dashboard.
+
For guidance, see <<view>>.
. From the dashboard's header, click image:more-menu-vert-ellipsis.png["",15,15] (*More menu*), and select *Delete*.
. Confirm the deletion.

== Manage Custom Dashboard Charts

Manage a chart from its image:more-menu-vert-ellipsis.png["",15,15] (*More menu*).

image::dashboard-custom-chart-more-hf.png["Chart More menu"]

* Collapse or expand the chart.
* Configure the chart:
** xref:dashboard-custom-config-graph.adoc[]
** xref:dashboard-custom-config-singlestat.adoc[]
** xref:dashboard-custom-config-table.adoc[]
** xref:dashboard-custom-config-text.adoc[]
* Duplicate the chart.
* Add or edit a graph's alert. Other chart types don't support alerts.
* Export graph or table data to a CSV file. Other chart types don't support CSV exports. 
+
For more information, see xref:export-app-data-to-csv.adoc[].
* Delete the chart.
