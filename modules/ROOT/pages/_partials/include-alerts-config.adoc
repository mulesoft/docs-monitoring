[[configure]]
=== Configure an Alert

. Navigate to the *Alert* configuration tab for a graph that is configured in basic mode.
. Click *Add Alert*.
. Use *Alert Config* to configure the alert. 
+
[%header,cols="1,4"]
|===
2+| Settings for Alerts in Custom Dashboards
| Name | A title for your alert
| Severity level | The severity level of your alert.
| `Condition` | WHEN: For triggering an  alert when the metric being measured is above a specified level
|===
. Configure email notifications when an alert is triggered. 
+
[%header,cols="1,4"]
|===
2+| Notification Settings for Alerts
| `Send To` | Email address for each recipient of the alert notification. If you add or change the email address of an alert recipient, they don't receive an email until the alert is triggered.
| `Message` | Message to send to the recipient.
|===
. Click *Save Changes*.
. Click `X` to exit the configuration and return to the dashboard.

[[state_history]]
=== View Alert State History

Use *State history* to view the status of the last fifty alerts.

You can also clear the history.

[[delete_alert]]
=== Delete an Alert

Use *Delete* to remove the alert from the graph.
