= Downloading Archived Log Data
:page-info-pills: anypoint-pricing-titanium

Download archived log data (raw data) collected on Mule apps that are running in hybrid, CloudHub, or Runtime Fabric deployments. This data is helpful for security, compliance, or other purposes when you need to examine or collect your warehoused data.

image::raw-data.png["Raw Data downloads in Anypoint Monitoring"]

Log data is archived regularly.


== Download Raw Log Data

include::partial$include-nav-steps-no-permissions.adoc[]

. In the sidebar, click *Raw Data*. 
. Select the *Environment* and *Resource*.
. Provide the date for the data you want to download, for example: `07/31/2026`.
. Locate and download log data that you want to examine.

== Troubleshoot

These factors impact the availability of data on the selected date:

* The app must be connected and running.
* Your subscription must support this feature.
